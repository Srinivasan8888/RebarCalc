# Competitive landscape: BBS / rebar detailing and estimation software (India first, global second)

Research date: 2026-11-03. Prices are quoted in the currency and on the date the source showed them. Anything older than 2024 is marked. Most Indian vendors publish no prices; "quote only" is a finding, not a gap.

## Key question 1: What is AutoScan Steel / Newton India? Product, pricing, customers, reviews, age, web version

### Takeaway
"AutoScan Steel" does not exist under that name. Newton Software Pvt Ltd (Pune, not Chennai; incorporated 2005, claims roots to 1997) sells two desktop AutoCAD add-ons: **AutoScan** (BOQ from CAD drawings to Excel) and **AutoSteel** (cutting lengths + BBS from CAD/RCC drawings, filtered into Excel). Both are quote-priced Windows desktop products with essentially no public reviews and no web version anywhere in the vendor's own material.

### Inferences
- The incumbent being "web-ified" is a 10+ year old AutoCAD add-on sold by demo and WhatsApp to Pune/Mumbai developers. Its moat is relationships and Excel-output habit, not product; review footprint is near zero on every marketplace.
- Newton's own listings already badge AutoSteel as "Online" on IndiaMART, so a web version is a plausible roadmap claim but there is no public evidence of one shipping.
- "Chennai" in the brief is likely a confusion with CADS Software India (Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai), the RebarCAD vendor.

### Gaps
- No public price for AutoSteel or AutoScan from any reliable source; the ₹1,900 Capterra figure is unverified and probably wrong (could be per-month, per-drawing, or a placeholder).
- No user counts, revenue, or seat numbers for Newton; Tofler financials not fetched.
- Could not confirm whether AutoSteel imports an existing BBS workbook (RebarCalc's core trick); the 2013 description is one-way CAD → Excel.

## Key question 2: Indian BBS tools: desktop, SaaS, mobile apps, Excel templates; INR pricing and licensing

### Takeaway
The Indian market splits into (a) AutoCAD add-ons from two old vendors (CADS RebarCAD India, Newton AutoSteel), (b) a Mumbai standalone (Ensoft BarBeQue, est. 1996), (c) cut-and-bend plant ERPs (Bendplus), (d) a handful of thin cloud/mobile tools (Reinforcement BBS, Yojo, Indus Developer app, Constropedia/Binary & Bricks), and (e) ₹99 to ₹999 Excel templates and ₹200 to ₹350 per tonne BBS-as-a-service. Almost nobody publishes a licence price; the only INR software prices found were a reseller's ₹1,01,000/yr for Exact Rebar and Tekla tiers via Techjockey.

### Inferences
- There is no Indian product that takes a customer's issued BBS workbook, recomputes it, and writes it back in the same layout. Every tool found is either CAD-in/Excel-out (RebarCAD, AutoSteel, Ali-Beam), form-in/PDF-out (apps, Yojo), or plant ERP (Bendplus imports Excel BBS but for production, not for QS checking).
- The price anchor for Indian buyers is ₹0 (Excel template) to ₹200-350/tonne (outsourced). A SaaS priced per project or per tonne, not per seat, would map onto how customers already pay.
- The only cloud "replace Excel" competitor (Reinforcement BBS) is a one-office Mumbai outfit with no published pricing or customers; the category is open.

### Gaps
- No INR list price for RebarCAD India, AutoSteel, BarBeQue, Reinforcement BBS, or Bendplus; all quote-only. The ₹1,01,000/yr Exact Rebar price came from a search snippet and was not visible on the fetched page.

## Key question 3: Global players: product, positioning, pricing

### Takeaway
Global rebar software is BIM/CAD-anchored (Tekla, Allplan, Revit add-ons, CADS, aSa, ProtaStructure) and priced at ₹1 lakh to ₹6 lakh per seat per year or more; none target an Indian QS who lives in a client-issued Excel. The new money is in AI takeoff (Beam AI, Togal, Kreo, Rudus, iRebar), priced $299/user/month to $8,000/yr or per-takeoff, aimed at North American estimators.

### Inferences
- Global BIM tools are 10-60x the price an Indian site office pays for anything, and all presume the rebar is modelled first. RebarCalc's input (an issued BBS or schedule in Excel) is upstream of nothing and downstream of everything, which is why none of these compete directly.
- The AI-takeoff cohort is converging on human-in-the-loop and "estimator stays in control" messaging (Rudus, iRebar, Beam AI done-for-you). RebarCalc's "doubts" panel is the same idea applied to schedule interpretation rather than drawing reading.
- iRebar's 2-3 day Excel delivery and +91 number suggests an India-based service shop wrapping AI; it is the nearest analogue to an Indian AI-BBS play and it chose per-takeoff pricing, not seats.

### Gaps
- Official Tekla and Allplan India prices not obtained (reseller and snippet only).
- aSa current pricing and any 2024-26 product news not found (site unreachable).
- No evidence either way on whether Candy/CostX/Bluebeam are used for BBS in India.

## Key question 4: Which tools do Excel round-trip, cutting-stock optimisation, AI drawing extraction, mobile, cloud collaboration

### Takeaway
Excel *export* is universal; Excel *import* exists only in plant ERPs (Bendplus) and generic Revit workflows; no tool found writes back into the customer's own layout. Cutting-stock optimisation is standard in detailing tools (RebarCAD, BarBeQue, Bendplus). AI extraction from PDFs is a 2025-26 wave priced for North America. Mobile is calculators only. Cloud collaboration is thin (Rebar Web, Reinforcement BBS, Bendplus portals).

### Inferences
- Table stakes in India (2026): IS 2502 shapes and bend deductions, d²/162 weights, dia-wise summary, Excel or PDF out, member types footing/column/beam/slab, and a free trial. Every app and add-on has these.
- Differentiators still open: round-trip into the client's own workbook; explicit flagged assumptions ("doubts"); recompute from a *schedule* (no spans) rather than a drawing; browser-only with no AutoCAD seat; per-project pricing.
- Cutting-stock optimisation is expected by fabricators but not by QS/billing engineers; it is a later add-on for RebarCalc, not a launch blocker.

### Gaps
- No vendor documents a "preserve customer layout" feature, but absence of marketing is not proof of absence; RebarCAD's "formatted to your preferences" claim may cover custom export templates.

## Key question 5: Cut-and-bend providers (Tata Tiscon ReadyBuild, JSW Neosteel Fastbuild, Jindal Panther, SAIL, Shyam Steel): do they bundle BBS software?

### Takeaway
The big mills sell BBS as a *service* bundled with cut-and-bend steel, not as software. Customers send drawings; the mill drafts the BBS, gets approval, then fabricates. No mill publishes which software it uses or offers a customer-facing BBS tool.

### Cited Findings
- Tata Steel ReadyBuild: downstream rebar offering with cut-and-bend and a "centralised BBS service that drafts schedules from customers' structural drawings"; Readybuild Centres across India; 8-40 mm; couplers sold only bundled with cut-and-bend; no software named. — [ReadyBuild brochure](https://readybuild.tatasteel.com/content/dam/tatasteelltdaemprogram/readybuild/brochure/Cut-Bend-Rebars-Brochure.pdf); [product page](https://readybuild.tatasteel.com/product.html)
- JSW Neosteel Fastbuild: ready-to-use cut-and-bend in Fe500D/Fe550D; brochure claims the industry uses "more than 90% of TMT rebars in cut & bend form" (vendor claim, undated). — [JSW Fastbuild](https://www.jswneosteel.in/products/Fastbuild.aspx); [JSW brochure](https://www.jswneosteel.in/Fastbuild/JSW_Neosteel_new28.pdf)
- Jindal Panther: ready-to-use cut-and-bend rebars, pitch is lower inventory, less wastage, labour saving; no software. — [jindalpanther.com](https://www.jindalpanther.com/cut-and-bend-rebar)
- Independent fabricators describe the workflow: customer provides drawings, fabricator makes BBS, customer approves, then manufacturing. — [Agarwal Steel Structures](https://www.steel-technology.com/products/agarwal-steel-structures-india-pvt-ltd/cut-bend)
- Market context: India steel rebar market USD 8,465.7 million in 2024 (IMARC); TMT 50-60% of Indian steel output, ~52 Mt in 2022-23 (Construction Times). — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-steel-rebar-market); [Construction Times](https://constructiontimes.co.in/tmt-steel-bars-the-workhorse-of-indian-construction-industry)

### Inferences
- The mill's BBS is produced by the seller of the steel, so the buyer-side QS has an incentive to independently check it. A tool that re-derives an issued BBS and flags discrepancies is a buyer-side audit tool, which no mill will supply.
- Mill BBS teams and ReadyBuild Centres are themselves a possible channel or customer (hundreds of BBS drafters producing Excel every day), separate from developers and contractors.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on SAIL or Shyam Steel cut-and-bend/BBS offerings.
- Which software ReadyBuild / Fastbuild BBS cells use internally is unpublished.
- The ">90% cut-and-bend" figure is a JSW marketing claim with no independent study; a credible 2025 share for India was not found.

## Key question 7: Table-stakes vs differentiating (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Table stakes: IS 2502 shapes, bend deductions, d²/162 weight, dia-wise totals, footing/column/beam/slab, Excel/PDF out, free trial. Differentiating in India today: ingest and re-emit the client's own workbook, flagged assumptions, schedule-to-BBS generation without CAD, browser-only, per-project price. Not yet expected by QS buyers but expected by fabricators: cutting-stock optimisation and machine files.

### Cited Findings
- Table-stakes features are present even in free tools (IS 2502/IS 13920 formulas, 7 shapes, cutting length and weight). — [engineeringa2z](https://tools.engineeringa2z.com/bar-bending-schedule-generator/); [Yojo](https://yojoapp.com/en/tools/bar-bending-schedule-generator/)
- Incumbent differentiators are CAD-side: bar drawing with code checks, automatic revision, macros, couplers, machine export. — [cads.co.uk](https://cads.co.uk/?p=16374)
- "Replace Excel" is the explicit positioning of the only Indian cloud competitor. — [reinforcement-bbs.in](https://www.reinforcement-bbs.in/)
- Human-in-the-loop / estimator-stays-in-control is the positioning of the 2026 AI entrants. — [Rudus HN](https://nuxt-hackernews-elgap-me.pages.dev/item/48374528); [iRebar](https://irebar-ai.vercel.app)
- Fabricator-side expectations: optimisation, tags, inventory, GRN, QR tracking. — [bendplus](https://bendplus.com/reinforcement-steel-software/)

### Inferences
- RebarCalc should position as "keep Excel, make it exact" rather than "replace Excel"; the one cloud rival chose the opposite and has no visible traction.
- The "doubts" concept has no equivalent in any Indian tool found and matches where the AI-takeoff market is heading; it is worth leading with.
- Earliest paying segments, by evidence of budget: developer estimation departments (Newton's customer list), EPC/large contractors (CADS's L&T, Tata Projects), and BBS service shops / mill BBS cells (ReadyBuild, Fastbuild) that produce Excel BBS all day.

### Gaps
- No win/loss or churn data on any incumbent; no interview evidence of what Indian QS teams actually pay for AutoSteel or RebarCAD seats.

# Voice of the customer: how Indian / South Asian / Gulf QS, billing and site engineers produce and use BBS, and what hurts

Scope note: web evidence collected 2026-11-03. Reddit and Quora blocked direct fetches (403 / HTML shell), so verbatim community quotes come mostly from Civil4M (Indian site-engineering forum), Play Store reviews, vendor blogs written by Indian practitioners, job descriptions and tender/spec PDFs. Where a source is a vendor, it is labelled. Material older than 2023 is flagged with its year.

## Q1. How is BBS prepared in India today, by whom, checked by whom, and where does it sit in the RA-bill cycle?

### Takeaway
BBS is overwhelmingly built in Excel from the structural drawings by the contractor's site/billing/BBS engineer; it is approved by the client or Engineer-in-charge, read by fitters in the yard, and then becomes the "theoretical consumption" baseline for steel reconciliation at every RA bill. Dedicated software (RebarCAD/CADS, BarBeQue) shows up only at large EPC firms (L&T, Tata Projects, Shapoorji) and in the Gulf.

### Inferences
- The role that owns BBS in India is a hybrid "Billing / QS & BBS engineer" at Rs 25k–80k/month, judged on client-bill submission and steel reconciliation; BBS is an input to the RA bill, not a standalone deliverable. Excel proficiency is a listed job requirement; BBS software never is (outside Gulf/EPC).
- The approval chain is: contractor engineer prepares (Excel) -> client / PMC / Engineer-in-charge approves -> yard cuts -> issued-vs-BBS reconciliation at each pour or each RA bill. Any tool must output something the approver can check "bar-by-bar".
- Dedicated software has penetrated only where detailing is done upstream of site (EPC design offices, Gulf detailers using RebarCAD/CADS RC to BS 8666).

## Q2. Common complaints: time, errors, lap/anchorage disputes, format mismatch, revisions, reconciliation, wastage, doubts raised to consultants

### Takeaway
The recurring pains in practitioner writing are: BBS is "the most tedious" quantity task; bend deductions, anchorage/Ld at supports and laps are the error and dispute hotspots; wastage against the BBS is argued at every bill (allowances 2–5%, measured reality 5–9%); drawing revisions force a redo; and manual Excel misses laps/deductions. Direct evidence of "format mismatch" and "40D vs 50D" disputes on the open web is thin; it is better evidenced by the project's own client workbooks.

### Cited Findings
Time and tedium
- "Preparation of reinforcement bar bending schedules for RCC work at construction sites is the most tedious" (Shapoorji training deck, c. 2015). — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/practical-training-at-shapoorji-pallonji-amp-co/56479481)
- Software Informer listing for an Excel BBS macro: building a rebar schedule "takes far longer than a typical quantity takeoff, mainly because of cutting lengths and bend deductions" and "late drawing revisions can force the whole calculation to be redone" (paraphrase in search summary). — [software.informer](https://bar-bending-schedule-program-in-microsof.software.informer.com/)
- Vendor copy aimed squarely at the pain: "Stop Wasting Time on Excel BBS Calculations"; "Replace Excel for faster & accurate BBS"; "Reduce reliance on inexperienced billing engineers"; "speed up billing approvals"; "Designed for Indian construction projects, contractors, and billing engineers". — [reinforcement-bbs.in (Mumbai vendor)](https://www.reinforcement-bbs.in/)

Errors in cut length / bend deduction / Ld
- InfraLens common mistakes: "Forgetting bend deductions" (most frequent fresher error), "Using nominal cover instead of clear cover", "Rounding individual bars instead of totals"; on anchorage at supports: "This is where most steel-quantity disputes during billing originate"; omitting it "makes your steel order short by a few percent on every project, and the contractor will (correctly) raise a variation." — [infralens.in](https://infralens.in/knowledge/bar-bending-schedule-tutorial-is-2502)
- Reinforcement-BBS (vendor, Mumbai) on reconciliation: "Manual Excel tracking ... often misses bend deductions and duplicate laps and fails to optimize cutting lists"; causes of variance listed as "theft, excessive cutting loss, billing errors, and fabrication mistakes"; normal variance "within 2–4%", red flag "above 5%". — [reinforcement-bbs.in steel reconciliation guide](https://www.reinforcement-bbs.in/post/steel-reconciliation-made-simple-a-practical-step-by-step-guide-for-site-engineers-contractors)
- CQRA (auditor) lists contractor BBS errors: "reinforcement claimed for members not reflected in approved drawings"; "spacing or bar diameters that deviate from design specifications"; "rounding errors in bar counts that repeat across multiple pours or floors"; duplicate claims across adjacent elements; non-compliant development lengths; "If an error appears once, check whether it has been carried forward across similar structural elements or levels." — [blog.cqra.com](https://blog.cqra.com/bar-bending-schedule-verification/)
- Civil4M Excel template thread: user "manish3012" (Apr 2018): "if i am delete 1 row, shape not delete." Author reply: "Its a problem of how excel sheets works." Lap / anchorage rule disputes (IS 456 vs client)
- Web guides contradict each other on lap values: one says slabs "generally 50d"; another "tension members = 40d (specially used for beam)" and "compression members = 50d (specially used for column)"; a third says lap depends on code, Ld, bar dia, concrete grade and stress state. — [CivilDigital](https://civildigital.com/calculation-lap-length-reinforced-concrete-structures/); [Engineering Discoveries, 2019](https://www.engineeringdiscoveries.net/2019/06/difference-between-lapping-length-and.html); [Daily Civil](https://dailycivil.com/difference-development-length-lap-length-1/)
- CPWD-style spec: only laps "as approved by the Owner/Architects or shown on drawings shall be paid for." — [Kolli Associates](https://kolliassociates.wordpress.com/quantity-surveying/technical-specifications-for-civil-building-works-as-per-cpwd/)
- InfraLens: "A 12-metre bar in a 25-metre column needs at least one lap; that is 50d × diameter of extra steel per lap." — [infralens.in](https://infralens.in/knowledge/bar-bending-schedule-tutorial-is-2502)

Wastage and reconciliation arguments
- Sitely (Indian site-ops SaaS founder, 2 May 2026) on a G+18 Thane tower with ~1,150 t steel: director believed wastage was 3% but "3 percent is what his estimator adds in the BOQ, not what his site actually consumed"; "Nobody measured anything, the figure is an allowance that got repeated until it became a belief"; reconciled "against the bar bending schedule" it "was closer to 8.4 percent"; "Steel usually lands between 5 and 9 percent against the theoretical bar bending schedule"; "Anything under 4 percent normally means somebody is either measuring it wrong or quietly counting offcuts as issued to another activity"; "Half the wastage arguments in this industry are actually two people measuring two different things"; "The bending yard was working strictly off whichever schedule the engineer handed over that morning," so "nesting across activities never happened." — [sitely.in](https://sitely.in/blog/construction-material-wastage)
- Civil4M wastage thread (Nov 2017, Dnyan Deshmukh): "Reinforcement Bars should be cut and bend only in quantity specified on Bar Bending Schedule"; fitters paid by output make extra stirrups, "Steel is free, so it does not make him to worry"; proposed PO clause "Material received beyond overweight tolerance limit will be debited to you"; reply (Srinivasan, Prime CE, Mumbai): "this condition is not acceptable by any vendor"; reply (natarajan.balu, Bangalore, Aug 2019): client should insist on reconciliation at every bill and "should penalise the contractor with heavy amount"; management rarely supports site staff and a staff member "is often removed at the end of the project." — [Civil4M thread 1075](https://civil4m.com/threads/how-to-control-reinforcement-steel-wastage-at-construction-projects.1075/)
- Civil4M "STEEL RECONCILLATION" (Jun 2019, Coimbatore apprentice): "in my site steel reconcillation was done by engineer and we found 26MT steel was found missing." Reply method: prepare detailed BBS, check challans vs weighbridge, apply IS 1786 rolling margin (±7% 8–10 mm, ±5% 12–16 mm, ±3% 20 mm+), apply allowable wastage; "Reconciliation of reinforcement= Material procured and received at site - Material used actually at site as per BBS". — [Civil4M thread 7547](https://civil4m.com/threads/steel-reconcillation.7547/)
- Civil4M rolling margin while billing (Sep 2018): "Quantity of steel after rolling margin is used for reconciliation and billing of contractor"; 10 t received 3% underweight -> supplier paid on 10 MT, contractor billed on 10.3 MT; "we will ask contractor about lost quantity of steel which cant be reconcile"; three later users asked "Send if u have any format using rolling mergin in reconciliation". — [Civil4M thread 5722](https://civil4m.com/threads/how-to-use-rolling-margin-while-billing.5722/)
- Indian QS blog (Aug 2021): allowable wastage "as per contract terms"; recovery "at the average market price + 15%" in one case; rolling margin "can play a very tricky part"; when "the recovery is pushed to a negative due to the rolling margin calculations" it "will have to be concluded based on a mutual agreement between the Client and the Contractor." — [The Indian Quantity Surveyor](https://theindianquantitysurveyor.blogspot.com/2019/08/reconciliation-of-materials-in.html)
- Wastage allowance figures conflict across sources: 2–5% (LinkedIn post, Tamal Sen), 5–10% (Arka Roy), 3% "Industry / CPWD; NSDC" (InfraLens table), "6-8%" without BBS vs "under 3%" with (Sitesetu, vendor). — [LinkedIn Tamal Sen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tamalsen/); [LinkedIn Arka Roy](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/download-bar-bending-schedule-box-culvert-arka-roy); [infralens wastage factors](https://infralens.in/knowledge/construction-material-wastage-factors-india); [sitesetu.in](https://sitesetu.in/blog/reduce-material-wastage-construction)

Revisions
- "If drawings have revisions, BBS must be updated. Otherwise you cut steel for an old design"; "The key is version control so the site always follows the latest schedule"; "If teams start cutting 'as per site feel' instead of bar marks, BBS benefits disappear"; "we prefer BBS-led planning so the client sees the logic behind steel consumption, not only the bill value" (Shelke Constructions, Pune contractor blog, c. mid-2026). — [shelkeconstructions.in](https://shelkeconstructions.in/bar-bending-schedule-bbs-explained-how-it-controls-steel-waste-and-cost/)
- Rebar revision practice (US detailer blog, for comparison): detailer "compares the revised drawings with the previous version and identifies affected bar marks"; fabricators "must then check which bars are already cut, bent, or completed". — [strand-co.com](https://strand-co.com/blogs/rebar-revision-management-drawings-bar-lists-fabrication)

Doubts to consultants
- RFI triggers in rebar detailing: "missing reinforcement details such as sizes and spacing", congested connections; after a response "the detailing team must apply it consistently to all affected rebar placing drawings, bar bending schedules, material takeoffs"; "RFIs may delay rebar detailing when work cannot continue without structural clarification." — [strand-co.com RFIs](https://strand-co.com/blogs/managing-rfis-during-rebar-detailing)
- Civil4M: "For dimensions of bends follow structural drawings provided by structural consultant"; if drawings lack dimensions, fall back to IS 2502 Figs 1A/1B. — [Civil4M thread 1091](https://civil4m.com/threads/how-to-prepare-bar-bending-schedule-and-its-usage-with-excel-sheet.1091/)

### Inferences
- The kilogram that gets argued is the reconciliation kilogram (issued vs BBS theoretical), not the BBS itself; a BBS tool earns trust when its totals survive the client's reconciliation and audit "bar-by-bar", which is why matching the client's own cut-rule (CEILING vs STD, Ld table) matters more than being "IS-correct".
- "Doubts" in the Indian context are mostly missing dimensions, bundled call-outs and lap locations; practitioners resolve them by falling back to IS 2502 or asking the consultant, and the web shows no tool that records those assumptions. RebarCalc's inline doubts are a differentiator with no visible competitor.
- Format-mismatch pain is not written about publicly because each firm treats its sheet as internal; the only visible proxy is repeated requests for "format" on forums and the RebarCAD testimonial praising "three types of report format".

### Gaps
- No public, dated case of a 40D-vs-50D or STD-vs-CEILING dispute between a contractor and a developer; the project's own DRA workbook (40D table under a 50D header) is the only hard evidence, and it is internal.
- No quantified "hours per floor" figure for BBS preparation from any source; only qualitative "most tedious" / "far longer than takeoff".
- Reddit r/civilengineering / r/StructuralEngineering threads could not be retrieved (blocked); Quora returned 403.

### Gaps
- Could not read YouTube comments or like counts (not in scraped page data); no Hindi-language Telegram group confirmed.

## Q4. Do developers/PMCs mandate specific BBS formats or software? Tender clauses?

### Takeaway
Public tenders require contractor-prepared BBS to be approved (or at least submitted for reference) and pay only on approved laps/chairs; none found mandate a software or a column layout. Large developers and EPCs are documented as users (not mandators) of RebarCAD/CADS RC and BarBeQue; Sobha's Dubai arm asks BBS engineers for RebarCAD skills.

### Cited Findings
- CPWD-style spec: contractor "shall prepare bar bending schedule of reinforcement" and "obtain approval of the Owner/Architects.(IS-2503)". — [Kolli Associates](https://kolliassociates.wordpress.com/quantity-surveying/technical-specifications-for-civil-building-works-as-per-cpwd/)

- Meghalaya DIT SCC (2022): approval of shop drawings/schedules "shall be an approval of general details and arrangements only". — [ditmeghalaya.gov.in](https://ditmeghalaya.gov.in/pdf/SCC_VOL_III_22_10_2022.pdf)

- Vendor (CADS) claims Sobha used RebarCAD/CADS RC in India and the Middle East; L&T produced "70 drawings and BBS" on an ONGC job; Sobha Constructions LLC Dubai JD asks for a BBS engineer working in "AutoCAD or RebarCAD". — [constructiontechnology.in](https://www.constructiontechnology.in/products/Technologies_Digitilization_Software/software-for-rebar-detailing-and-automatic-bar-bending-schedule); [jobleads Sobha Dubai](https://www.jobleads.com/ae/job/bar-bending-schedule-engineer-rcc-autocad-rebar--dubai--ec285bbd6b58a0d5239c160c58ae0059c)

### Inferences
- Format is enforced socially (the client's billing engineer rejects what he cannot check in his own sheet), not contractually. This matches the hypothesis "each developer has a house format" but the web cannot prove it; the two client workbooks in `data/` are the strongest evidence available.
- Because approval is "of general details only" and wastage/unauthorised laps are unpaid, the contractor carries the risk of a wrong BBS; that is the buying motive on the contractor side. On the developer side the motive is over-billing prevention (CQRA's pitch).

### Gaps
- No Provident/Puravankara, DRA, Prestige, Sobha India, L&T or Shapoorji contractor manual or tender clause specifying a BBS template or software was found online.

### Takeaway

## Q7. Adjacent jobs the same user does

### Takeaway
The BBS engineer's week is RA bills, joint measurement, dia-wise steel reconciliation (with rolling margin), indents by diameter, cutting lists for the yard and offcut/scrap tracking, plus concrete and shuttering quantities; every one of these consumes the BBS totals by diameter.

### Cited Findings
- JD duties: "Preparation and Submission of Monthly Client Bill/ Sub Contractor billing"; "Reconciliation of Concrete / Steel/ Cement with the Bill" (Gina Engineering); "Preparation of RA bills and final bills", "Quantity takeoff from drawings", "Joint measurements with client/consultant", "Preparing BOQ comparison and rate analysis", "Subcontractor bill verification", "Maintaining DPR and WPR reports" (Goel); SPD Constructions (Odisha): coordinates "with site, billing, and planning teams on steel requirements and reconciliation", Rs 25,000–66,667/month; "preparing material reconciliation statements at every RA bill stage" (billing JD). — [Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/Bbs-Engineer-jobs); [Goel](https://goelconstruction.co.in/job/billing-engineer/); [bebee SPD](https://bebee.com/in/jobs/bbs-engineer-spd-constructions-berhampur-bhubaneswar-mayurbhanj-puri--theirstack-708820220); [talent.com](https://in.talent.com/view?id=090a85921acb)
- Reconciliation must be dia-wise: "every stage must be done dia-wise, never as a lump total" and must account for rolling margin and reusable cut pieces. — [civaropulse.com](https://civaropulse.com/steel-reconciliationt-complete-guide/)
- Rolling margin check: weigh 1 m samples per diameter monthly against IS unit weight; receipts logged "by diameter and heat number"; off-cuts > 1.0–1.5 m kept as stock, < 1.0 m weighed as scrap; "invisible wastage" 0.5–1.0%. — [reinforcement-bbs.in](https://www.reinforcement-bbs.in/post/steel-reconciliation-made-simple-a-practical-step-by-step-guide-for-site-engineers-contractors)
- Cutting lists / nesting: "If you cut a 12 metre bar into two 5.3 metre pieces you have thrown away 1.4 metres"; fix was "issuing steel against a cut plan that covered 3 activities at once instead of 1" plus a "labelled offcut stack that had to be consumed before fresh bars were released", moving wastage "from roughly 8.4 percent to about 6.1 percent over 5 months"; tracking loop: "Define the theoretical requirement per activity from the BOQ and the bar bending schedule ... Compare actual to theoretical the same week, not the same year ... Split the variance into cutting loss, over-consumption, damage and rework." — [sitely.in](https://sitely.in/blog/construction-material-wastage)
- Stock lengths 12,000 mm (Tata Tiscon, JSW Neo), 3 mm kerf, 3–5% waste target with an optimised cutting list. — [metalweightpro.com](https://www.metalweightpro.com/articles/rebar-cutting-waste-reduction.html)
- Ordering: BBS lets steel be ordered "in planned lots"; "BBS-led planning so the client sees the logic behind steel consumption". — [shelkeconstructions.in](https://shelkeconstructions.in/bar-bending-schedule-bbs-explained-how-it-controls-steel-waste-and-cost/)
- BBS "should be grouped member-wise to match the running-bill abstract format". — [civilos.in](https://civilos.in/guides/bar-bending-schedule.html)
- Scrap outcomes claimed by software users: "scrap as low as 2%, when it was as high as 10% before" (OMPL/L&T, vendor testimonial); Tata Projects "efficiently use the off cuts". — [cadsindia.com](https://www.cadsindia.com/rebarcad/)
- Reconciliation and RA bill templates co-exist with BBS templates on the same Indian template sites (Sitesetu RA bill with "previous, current and cumulative quantities" and "deduction reconciliation"; InstaBOQ RA bill template). — [sitesetu.app templates](https://sitesetu.app/resources/templates); [instaboq](https://www.instaboq.com/templates/bar-bending-schedule/)
- Interview/role framing: "Role of Billing Engineer as a Civil Engineer in Indian Construction Industry" (Bhadani's training). — [bhadanisrecordedlectures.com](https://www.bhadanisrecordedlectures.com/blog/role-of-billing-engineer-as-a-civil-engineer-in-indian-construction-industry)

### Inferences
- The natural next exports after the BBS sheet are: dia-wise TOTAL per pour/floor (already in RebarCalc), a dia-wise indent, and a reconciliation statement (issued vs BBS theoretical with rolling margin and allowed %). Cutting optimisation is the step the yard and the wastage argument both want but no Excel does.
- "Member-wise grouping matching the RA abstract" is a format requirement that argues for keeping the client's row order on export.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies how long reconciliation takes per RA bill or how often it is disputed; only anecdotes (26 MT missing; 3% believed vs 8.4% measured).
- Gulf-specific adjacent workflow (BS 8666 shape codes, fabricator barcode tags via RebarSoft/Schnell/PEDAX exports) is documented only on vendor pages: [rebarsoft.net](https://rebarsoft.net/); [careermidway Qatar JD](https://www.careermidway.com/jobs/doha/rebar-detailer-civil-engineer/301976) ("Proficiency in CADS RC or RebarCAD or AutoCAD").

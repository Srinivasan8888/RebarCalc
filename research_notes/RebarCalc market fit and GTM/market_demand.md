# Market size and demand signals for BBS / rebar estimation software (India primary, Gulf/South Asia secondary)

Research date: 3 Nov 2026. Figures carry their own year. "Paid report" = market-research vendor estimate (treat as order-of-magnitude only). "Vendor" = software/steel seller marketing.

## Q1. Indian construction output and TMT rebar consumption, with 2025-2030 forecasts

### Takeaway
India consumed ~152 MT of finished steel in FY25 and ~164 MT in FY26; the JPC "Bars & Rods" category (the closest official proxy for rebar) was 63.1 MT in FY25, growing ~10% a year. At FY26 mill prices of roughly Rs 50,000-65,000/t ex-GST, that is a Rs 3-4 lakh crore (~US$35-45 bn) annual rebar flow, every tonne of which passes through some form of BBS.

### Cited Findings
- Domestic finished steel consumption was 152.129 MT in 2024-25 (FY25) — [Ministry of Steel Annual Report 2025-26](https://steel.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/Final%20Annual%20Report%202025-26%20(English%20Version).pdf)
- JPC "Bars & Rods" consumption for April 2024-March 2025 was 63.067 MnT, up 10.0% year on year — [JPC Indian Steel Industry trend report, April 2025](https://jpcindiansteel.nic.in/writereaddata/files/TrendReportApril%202025.pdf)
- Long (non-flat) product consumption rose from 51.4 MT in FY21 to 81.1 MT in FY25; non-flat products are ~53% of total consumption (secondary write-up citing JPC data) — [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/ipo/india-steel-consumption-global-decline-rhp-cmvt4sm8n000u23pnk7gyhbmc)
- FY26: finished steel consumption 163.7 MT (Ministry of Steel rounds to 164 MT), finished steel production 160.9 MT, crude steel production 168.4 MT; crude capacity rose from 200 MT (FY25) to 220 MT (FY26); H1 FY27 (Apr-Sep 2026) consumption 85 Mt, +7.6% YoY — [IBEF steel industry page](https://www.ibef.org/industry/steel)
- World Steel Association forecast (calendar years): Indian steel demand +7.9% to 172.4 MT in 2026 and +8% to 186.2 MT in 2027 — [IBEF](https://www.ibef.org/industry/steel)
- National Steel Policy targets: 300 MT crude capacity by 2030-31 and 160 kg per-capita consumption; production projected 168.4 MT (FY26) to 255 MT by FY31; US$156 bn investment needed — [IBEF](https://www.ibef.org/industry/steel); Ministry reaffirmed 300 MT by 2030 and 400 MT by 2035 in March 2026 — [The Hawk](https://www.thehawk.in/news/economy-and-business/indias-steel-sector-aims-to-reach-300-million-tonnes-capacity-by-2030-govt-official)
- TMT bar consumption specifically: 50.3 MT in FY23 vs 46 MT in FY22 (industry magazine, no primary cited) — [Construction Times](https://constructiontimes.co.in/tmt-steel-bars-the-workhorse-of-indian-construction-industry)
- TMT market value Rs 1,37,946 crore in 2023, projected Rs 2,04,764 crore by 2030 (paid report / consultancy blog) — [Entrepreneur India](https://www.entrepreneurindia.co/blogs/india-tmt-bar-industry/)
- TMT demand split claims conflict: one source 60% infrastructure / 40% residential-commercial; a paid report says 33% infrastructure and 39% residential in 2025 — [The India Watch](https://theindiawatch.com/insights-new/infrastructure-and-real-estate/india-growing-tmt-bar-market); [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/tmt-steel-bar-market)
- Construction + real estate services + ownership of dwellings = ~14.3% of total economic output at constant prices, FY23 — [Industry report on Indian construction, March 2025 (PDF)](https://goelconstruction.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Industry-Report-on-Indian-Construction-Industry_05-March-2025_Final-Report.pdf)
- Construction market INR 25.31 trillion in 2025 (+11.2%), 8.8% CAGR 2025-29 (paid report) — [ResearchAndMarkets via BusinessWire, Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251118406333/en/India-Construction-Industry-Report-2025-Market-to-Grow-by-11.2-to-Reach-INR-25.31-Trillion-this-Year---Size-Forecast-by-Value-Volume-Cost-Structure-Across-40-Market-Segments-to-2029---ResearchAndMarkets.com)
- Construction market USD 645.92 bn (2025) to USD 985.22 bn (2031), 6.87% CAGR (paid report) — [TechSci](https://www.techsciresearch.com/report/india-construction-market/10568.html); USD 0.74 tn (2025) to USD 1.1 tn (2031) (paid report) — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-construction-market)
- Residential construction USD 263.89 bn (2025) to USD 387.51 bn (2031) (paid report) — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/india-residential-construction-market); Deloitte-attributed US$350 bn by 2030 (secondary) — [Ajmal IAS Academy](https://ajmaliasacademy.in/indias-residential-construction-industry-poised-to-reach-us350-billion-by-2030/)
- Real estate sector projected at US$1 trillion by 2030 (CREDAI-Colliers, cited secondhand) — [Ceramic World Web](https://ceramicworldweb.com/en/economics-and-markets/indian-construction-industry-offers-abundant-business-opportunities)
- TMT price, Sept 2026: Rs 50,000-65,000/tonne before 18% GST (aggregator) — [HeadsUpB2B](https://www.headsupb2b.com/blog/tmt-bar-price-today); Q1 2026 brand quotes ex-GST: JSW Neosteel Rs 54,000/MT, Tata Tiscon Rs 57,500/MT, SAIL Rs 45,600/MT — [Nexizo](https://nexizo.ai/blogs/tmt-bar-price-today-brand-wise-location-wise-and-grade-wise-updates); OfBusiness lists primary TMT Fe500D/550 at Rs 50-65/kg — [OfBusiness](https://www.ofbusiness.com/prices/mild-steel/primary-tmt)

### Inferences
- Rebar value flow: 63 MT (FY25 Bars & Rods) x Rs 55,000/t mid-point = ~Rs 3.5 lakh crore (~US$40 bn) per year. Scaling by FY26 steel growth (~7.6%) gives ~68 MT / Rs 3.7 lakh crore for FY26. This is the pool any wastage or accuracy saving is drawn from.
- "Bars & Rods" includes wire rod and some non-rebar long products, so pure TMT rebar is lower; the FY23 TMT figure of 50.3 MT is consistent with ~80% of Bars & Rods being TMT.
- Steel demand forecasts cluster at 7-9% a year through 2030, faster than GDP, so the BBS workload grows regardless of software adoption.
- Construction market estimates differ 2x by scope (USD 650 bn to 1.2 tn in 2025); use the Ministry of Steel and JPC tonnage as the anchor, not the paid-report dollar values.

### Gaps
- No official FY25 or FY26 rebar-only (TMT) tonnage; JPC reports "Bars & Rods" only. The JPC October 2025 trend report could not be fetched (server refused connection). Exact long-product share by end-use (building vs infra) is not published by JPC.
- No official value (Rs) for TMT consumption; all value figures are paid-report estimates.

Hard counts exist for developers (1.38-1.43 lakh RERA projects registered 2017-Jan 2025; CREDAI 13,000+ developers) but not for contractors, structural consultants, PMC/QS firms or fabricators; professional QS bodies in India are small (IAStructE had 760 members in 2015; ISSE ~1,684) and publish no current totals.

### Inferences
- Active project count is the better buyer proxy than firm count: ~1.4 lakh RERA projects over 8 years = ~17,000-18,000 new residential/commercial projects a year nationally, each needing a BBS per floor. Maharashtra (40%) plus Pune as top city makes western India the densest territory.
- With 13,000+ CREDAI developers plus their contractors, consultants and PMCs, a plausible organisation TAM for India is in the low tens of thousands; the Pune-district Udyam share (5-7% construction) applied to 7.83 crore MSMEs would imply millions of construction micro-enterprises, almost all too small or informal to buy software.
- Professional-body membership (hundreds to low thousands) is tiny relative to practitioner headcount; QS/billing engineering in India is largely uncredentialed, so distribution has to go through employers and training channels, not institutes.

## Q3. Labour-market demand: BBS engineers, billing engineers, quantity surveyors; freelance and outsourcing rates

### Inferences
- A BBS engineer at Rs 35,000/month costs ~Rs 1,600/working day. Software that saves one day per floor per month pays for itself at Rs 1,500-2,000/month per seat; pricing much above Rs 5,000/seat/month competes with simply hiring another engineer.
- The Fiverr floor (US$50 per BBS) is the price anchor for an "import schedule, export BBS" tool: a per-project or per-floor price under Rs 2,000 is defensible; per-seat SaaS at Western prices is not.
- Indian and Pakistani freelancers dominating Fiverr BBS gigs confirms the skill is commoditised and Excel-based, which is the layout RebarCalc already reads and writes.

## Q4. Financial stakes: steel share of RCC cost, wastage, cost of errors

### Takeaway
Steel is 3.5-5 kg/sq ft and 25-40% of RCC structure cost; the one peer-reviewed Indian case (Embassy Manyata, Bangalore, 2015) cut rebar wastage from 3.36% (manual BBS) to 0.53% with optimisation software, saving 23.42 t (Rs 12.9 lakh) on ~860 t of steel, with software cost of Rs 2.3 lakh. Vendor and practitioner sources put un-scheduled sites at 5-8% wastage and disciplined BBS sites under 3%.

### Cited Findings
- Steel quantity: ~3.5-5 kg of TMT per sq ft built-up area for a typical RCC frame; up to 6 kg/sq ft in seismic zones IV-V; G+2 houses 4.5-6 kg/sq ft (estimator sites, 2026) — [Houseyog](https://www.houseyog.com/blog/steel-required-for-house-construction/); [Construction Estimator India](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/rcc-construction-cost-per-sq-ft-in-india/)
- Steel share of RCC cost: 25-35% in one estimator, 30-40% in another; structure ~56% of total civil cost — [Construction Estimator India RCC cost](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/what-is-the-cost-of-rcc-per-square-foot-with-material/); [Comaron 2026 guide](https://www.comaron.com/blog/rcc-frame-structure-cost-per-sq-ft-india-2026-complete-guide)
- One estimator arrives at ~Rs 232/sq ft for steel at 4 kg/sq ft x Rs 58/kg — [Aecord](https://aecord.com/blog/rcc-rate-per-sq-ft-with-material-2)
- Embassy Manyata Building G4, Bangalore (study published 2015): manual BBS average wastage 3.36%; RGS Rebar software 0.53%; 23.42 MT steel saved; Rs 12,87,942.92 saved (Table 4) on 859.96 MT (manual) vs 836.54 MT (software) across 8 packages up to ground-floor roof; building total 4,690 MT; software implementation cost Rs 2,30,806 (AutoCAD Rs 1,90,806 + RGS Rebar Rs 40,000) — [International Journal of Technology 5(2), 2015](https://ijtonline.com/HTML_Papers/International%20Journal%20of%20Technology__PID__2015-5-2-44.html)
- Indian site survey: actual rebar wastage ~1% above planned, concrete ~1.5%; infrastructure projects waste more than buildings — [ResearchGate: Planned and actual wastage of materials in Indian construction industry](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/359789693_A_STUDY_ON_PLANNED_AND_ACTUAL_WASTAGE_OF_MATERIALS_IN_INDIAN_CONSTRUCTION_INDUSTRY)
- Practitioner benchmark: site without BBS routinely 6-8% steel wastage, with disciplined BBS under 3% (vendor blog) — [BTTotek](https://www.bttotek.in/blog/material-wastage-control-construction-site-benchmarks); 3-5% acceptable target with an optimised cutting list — [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/blog/reduce-material-wastage-construction)
- Kumar Properties (Pune) case: rebar wastage below 0.5% per site, down from 3-5%, rebar stock cut 50% (vendor case study, Viskartech) — [Viskartech blog](https://www.viskartech.com/global/blog/how-kumar-properties-reduced-the-rebar-stock-levels-in-the-site-by-50-and-how-they-brought-down-rebar-wastage-to-a-minimum-of-0-5/)
- Lap-splice optimisation can bring cutting waste below 1% (non-India academic study, Nov 2024) — [Journal of Asian Architecture and Building Engineering via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/3f4c5615eb2043a39d95cff8e17977da)
- Tata Steel positions cut & bend against on-site cutting that "causes wastage" by "unskilled labours"; "wastage is a major headache as rebars do not come in required sizes" (vendor) — [Tata Readybuild brochure](https://readybuild.tatasteel.com/content/dam/tatasteelltdaemprogram/readybuild/brochure/Cut-Bend-Rebars-Brochure.pdf)
- TMT price Rs 50,000-65,000/t ex-GST, Sept 2026 — [HeadsUpB2B](https://www.headsupb2b.com/blog/tmt-bar-price-today)

### Inferences
- Per-project stakes: the Embassy Manyata building (4,690 t) at Rs 55,000/t is ~Rs 25.8 crore of rebar; a 2.8 percentage-point wastage cut is ~Rs 72 lakh on one building. A 1% error on the DRA 19th-floor beam BBS in `data/` (34.2 t) is ~Rs 19,000 per floor per beam package.
- National stakes: 1 percentage point of wastage on 63 MT is 630,000 t, ~Rs 3,500 crore a year. Even a 0.5 pp improvement on the ~20% of tonnage that flows through formal developers is a Rs 350 crore/yr pool.
- The Embassy study's 2015 software spend (Rs 2.3 lakh, of which AutoCAD was 83%) shows that a tool not needing a CAD host removes most of the cost barrier; the BBS tool itself was priced at Rs 40,000.
- Wastage figures below 1% come from vendors; the independent Indian survey shows only ~1% over plan. The honest claim for a BBS tool is "from 3-5% to about 1%", not "zero wastage".

### Gaps
- No independent Indian study quantifying the cost of BBS errors (re-cutting, over-ordering, billing disputes) as distinct from cutting wastage.
- CPWD's 3-5% steel wastage allowance is widely quoted but was not verified against a primary CPWD document.
- No 2025-26 Indian wastage survey; the peer-reviewed case is from 2015.

### Inferences
- Established Indian firms already pay ~Rs 1 lakh/seat/yr for AutoCAD and STAAD; a BBS tool at Rs 20,000-60,000/seat/yr (Rs 1,700-5,000/month) sits well inside the existing spend of a consultant or Class I contractor and below RGS Rebar's US$75 (~Rs 6,300)/month.
- The large unserved segment is Excel-only: site billing teams at Rs 25,000-40,000/month salaries with no CAD licence. For them a per-project or per-floor price (Rs 500-2,000) or a Rs 1,000/month seat matches the only observed Indian BBS price point and the Fiverr floor.
- Grey-market STAAD/AutoCAD listings at Rs 4,999 "lifetime" on Amazon.in signal heavy piracy tolerance in the small-contractor segment; a web app with server-side export is harder to pirate than a desktop tool.

### Gaps
- No Indian price found for Tekla Structures, Candy (RIB CCS), CostX, or RebarCAD.
- No survey data on software spend as a share of revenue for Indian small contractors; the "spend norm" question is unanswered except by inference from list prices.

## Q6. Policy tailwinds: capex, housing, BIM, prefab rebar

### Takeaway
Central capex is at a record Rs 12.2 lakh crore for FY27 (up from Rs 11.2 lakh crore FY26) with PMAY-Urban funding more than doubled to Rs 18,625 crore and 1.18 crore PMAY-U houses already sanctioned; there is no verified national BIM mandate, and the prefab/cut-and-bend trend is led by Tata and Jindal, which shifts BBS generation upstream to mills and fabricators.

### Cited Findings
- Union Budget 2026-27: public capex Rs 12.2 lakh crore, up ~9% from Rs 11.2 lakh crore in FY26; effective capital expenditure (incl. grants for asset creation) Rs 17.14 lakh crore — [Outlook Business, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/budget/budget-2026-fm-sitharaman-raises-capex-for-fy27-to-122-lakh-crore); [NBM&CW](https://www.nbmcw.com/news/union-budget-2026-continued-thrust-on-infra-development-technology-adoption-and-domestic-manufacturing.html)
- PMAY-Urban allocation Rs 18,625 crore for 2026-27 vs Rs 7,500 crore prior year; PMAY-U 2.0 Rs 3,000 crore (10x from Rs 300 crore); Urban Challenge Fund Rs 10,000 crore; AMRUT Rs 8,000 crore; Railways capex Rs 2,93,030 crore — [Rustomjee budget blog, Feb 2026](https://www.rustomjee.com/blog/budget-2026-27-overview-of-real-estate-and-infrastructure-provisions/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/02/01/full-analysis-how-budget-2026-27-paves-the-way-for-a-developed-india.amp.html)

- Economic Survey 2024-25: over 1.18 crore houses sanctioned under PMAY-Urban; PMAY-U 2.0 targets one crore homes — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/survey-says-1-18-crore-houses-sanctioned-under-pmay-urban/2472063/); [Rentu](https://rentu.in/property-news/1-38-lakh-projects-registered-under-rera-pmay-u-2-0-targets-one-crore-homes/1365)
- BIM: blogs claim a CPWD BIM mandate for projects above Rs 20 crore and NHAI above Rs 100 crore — [Primaversity](https://www.primaversity.com/post/complete-guide-to-government-bim-mandates-in-india-cpwd-nhai-smart-cities-projects-bim-mandate); contradicted by sources stating India has no national BIM mandate equivalent to the UK's — [JF India](https://www.jf-india.in/bim-adoption-in-indias-construction-industry-where-we-stand-and-why-it-matters-for-project-delivery/); NHAI has a BIM toolkit/guidelines; MoHUA references BIM in Smart City guidelines — [Novatr](https://www.novatr.com/blog/bim-in-india)
- Steel capacity target 300 MT by 2030 and 400 MT by 2035 reaffirmed March 2026 — [The Hawk](https://www.thehawk.in/news/economy-and-business/indias-steel-sector-aims-to-reach-300-million-tonnes-capacity-by-2030-govt-official)

### Inferences
- Public capex and PMAY-U drive the infrastructure and affordable-housing share of rebar, but these buyers (PSUs, state housing boards, their EPC contractors) procure on CPWD/state formats; an Excel-layout round-trip is the right wedge there because formats are mandated, not chosen.
- Cut-and-bend growth creates a second buyer class: the fabricator who needs an exact, machine-ready BBS from a customer's drawing or schedule. Tata and Jindal centres are prospects for the import/generate side of RebarCalc.
- BIM is not a near-term tailwind in India; the absence of a mandate means Excel BBS remains the deliverable of record for the forecast period.

### Gaps
- No primary CPWD or NHAI circular found confirming any BIM threshold; treat all mandate claims as unverified.
- PM Gati Shakti-specific allocations and e-invoicing/GST effects on BBS workflows: no relevant sources found.
- No PMAY-U 2.0 sanctioned-house count for 2025-26.

## Q7. Gulf / Middle East demand for BBS

### Takeaway
UAE rebar bookings hit a 2025 high above 470,000 t in August 2025 and Saudi demand is 10-15 Mt/yr depending on the (paid) source; GulfTalent carries 69 UAE QS jobs, 792 Saudi site engineer/QS jobs and 1,600-2,000 QS-engineer listings region-wide, and NaukriGulf lists 2,890 jobs for Indians, but no official data on the Indian share of Gulf QS roles was found.

### Cited Findings
- UAE, Aug 2025: Emirates Steel took 192,000 t of rebar orders; total bookings across UAE-serving mills (AGSI, Gulf Steel, Conares, Muscat Steel) exceeded 470,000 t, the highest monthly figure of 2025 to that point, on "a robust pipeline of upcoming construction projects" (Kallanish via SEAISI) — [SEAISI](https://www.seaisi.org/details/27092?type=news-rooms)
- UAE: residential is the largest construction share; 30-60+ storey towers need dense reinforcement; construction cycle shortened from 4+ years to 2.6 years between 2021 and 2025 — [GMK Center](https://gmk.center/en/posts/the-uae-steel-market-on-a-high-plateau/amp/)
- Saudi rebar demand 2025: 10.40 Mt (paid report) — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-steel-pipes-and-rebars-market); versus 14.5 Mt (2024) to 15.5 Mt (2025) (paid report) — [Mark Spark Solutions](https://marksparksolutions.com/reports/saudi-arabia-steel-rebar-market); sources conflict
- Qatar: a 500-800 sq m villa needs 40-70 t of rebar — [GMK Center Qatar](https://gmk.center/en/posts/the-qatari-steel-market-a-shift-in-focus/)
- Gulf buyers cautious, limiting spot purchases (2026 price report, paid) — [IMARC rebar pricing](https://www.imarcgroup.com/rebar-pricing-report)
- Live QS listings: 69 in UAE (Dubai largest), 792 site engineer + QS in Saudi (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dhahran) — [GulfTalent UAE](https://www.gulftalent.com/UAE/jobs/title/quantity-surveyor); [GulfTalent KSA](https://www.gulftalent.com/mobile/saudi-arabia/jobs/title/site-engineer-and-quantity-surveyor); NaukriGulf 2,890 jobs for Indians — [NaukriGulf](https://www.naukrigulf.com/jobs-for-indians-14); Saudi QS roles asking 3-12 years' experience — [NaukriGulf Saudi QS](https://www.naukrigulf.com/quantity-surveyor-jobs-in-saudi-arabia-in-discomb-group-3-to-12-years-n-cd-20010005-jid-241125500297)

- Claim that Indian nationals hold ~38% of Gulf construction roles and that Saudi MHRSD projects 2.5 million construction workers through 2030: single training-company blog, no primary source — [SkilledUp India blog](https://skilledupindia.pages.dev/blog/saudi-arabia-is-building-the-future-and-they-need-you)
- IIQS has a Qatar chapter, indicating organised Indian QS presence in the Gulf — [IIQS Qatar on X](https://x.com/iiqsqatar)
- Rebar detailing companies are concentrated in the Middle East and India — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebar_detailing)

### Inferences
- Gulf BBS work follows BS 8666 shape codes and contractor-specific Excel layouts rather than IS 2502; the Excel-layout round-trip approach ports, but the shape-code library and Ld/lap rules would need a BS 8666 mode before Gulf sales.
- The Gulf is better approached through Indian QS staff and Indian detailing outsourcers already serving it (Wikipedia's "majority in Middle East and India") than through direct Gulf sales; the DRA workbook already in `data/` is evidence of the Indian-firm-to-Gulf-format pipeline.

### Gaps
- No official statistics on the Indian share of Gulf QS/engineering employment (only one unsourced blog claim). UAE and Saudi labour-ministry data not retrieved.
- No data on BBS software adoption or pricing in the Gulf.
- UAE and Saudi annual rebar consumption figures are paid-report estimates and disagree by 50%; no primary (e.g., Emirates Steel annual report) figure retrieved.

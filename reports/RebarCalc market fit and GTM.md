# Sell exactness to the client who checks the kilograms

*Market fit, demand and go-to-market brief for RebarCalc. Research date 3 November 2026. Written for the GTM lead and the product manager. Sources are the four notes in `research_notes/RebarCalc market fit and GTM/`; every number below links to its origin.*

**Bottom line.** RebarCalc should be sold as the tool that makes a contractor's Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) survive the client's bar-by-bar check, not as a replacement for Excel. The buyer is the organisation whose kilograms get argued at every running-account (RA) bill: mid-market developers and project management consultants (PMCs) who issue a house BBS format, and the contractors who must bill in it. The pool is large and growing: India consumed **63.1 million tonnes of bars and rods in FY25**, up 10% ([JPC](https://jpcindiansteel.nic.in/writereaddata/files/TrendReportApril%202025.pdf)), roughly Rs 3.5 lakh crore of rebar a year, and sites land at **5 to 9% wastage against the theoretical BBS** while budgeting 2 to 3% ([Sitely](https://sitely.in/blog/construction-material-wastage)). No Indian tool imports a client's issued workbook, recomputes it exactly and writes it back in the same layout with assumptions flagged; the incumbents (Newton AutoSteel, CADS RebarCAD, Ensoft BarBeQue) are quote-only AutoCAD add-ons and the one "replace Excel" cloud rival shows no customers. Today the app has problem-solution fit in exactly two formats (Provident slab, DRA beam) and nothing beyond that: slabs and beams only, no login, no backend, no sync, no paying user. The next build items, in order, are columns and walls (the largest missing tonnage), a dia-wise reconciliation statement with rolling margin, a printable yard sheet, cut-plan optimisation and a persisted doubts log; AI drawing extraction is a 2027 bet now that a free Indian entrant exists, and BIM export should stay off the roadmap. Price per house format and per floor against the Rs 200 per tonne outsourcing anchor, publish the price, and win accounts one workbook at a time through the developer's head of estimation, not through individual engineers who are trained to pay nothing.

## The buyer is whoever has to defend the kilograms at the RA bill

In India the BBS is prepared in Excel by the contractor's billing, QS or BBS engineer, approved by the owner, PMC or Engineer-in-charge, handed to fitters as a printout, and then becomes the "theoretical consumption" baseline against which issued steel is reconciled at every RA bill. The CPWD-style specification used on private works says the contractor "shall prepare bar bending schedule of reinforcement" and "shall obtain approval of the Owner/Architects", pays only "nett measurements", and makes the contractor "allow in his quoted rates for all wastage" ([Kolli Associates](https://kolliassociates.wordpress.com/quantity-surveying/technical-specifications-for-civil-building-works-as-per-cpwd/)). Indian Railways conditions tie steel consumption to the "approved bar bending schedule" ([Sentinel SCC](https://images.assettype.com/sentinelassam-english/2024-09-12/01ow7wo0/151908847__1_.pdf)). On the client side, the quantity auditor CQRA tells developers to "never approve an RA bill based solely on the contractor's submitted BBS" and that "bar-by-bar verification, not sample checks, is the only way to catch systematic discrepancies" ([CQRA, Sep 2026](https://blog.cqra.com/bar-bending-schedule-verification/)). Job descriptions confirm the role: BBS engineers at Rs 25,000 to 80,000 a month are judged on "Preparation and Submission of Monthly Client Bill" and "Reconciliation of Concrete / Steel / Cement with the Bill", with "Strong MS Excel skills" required and BBS software never mentioned ([Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/Bbs-Engineer-jobs); [Goel Construction](https://goelconstruction.co.in/job/billing-engineer/)).

That chain defines the ideal customer. The decisive objection to any BBS tool is "will the client's billing engineer accept the numbers", and the only way to answer it is to reproduce the client's own cut rule, Ld table and column layout. That is what RebarCalc already does for two workbooks, which makes the **format owner** the natural first buyer and the contractor the natural second.

The three users in `PRODUCT.md` map onto this cleanly. The QS or estimator in the office is the daily user and, inside a developer, the buyer. The contractor's project manager is the economic buyer on the other side of the table and reads only totals. The site engineer and bar bender are read-only consumers, and the evidence says their artefact is paper: "the bending yard was working strictly off whichever schedule the engineer handed over that morning" ([Sitely](https://sitely.in/blog/construction-material-wastage)), and the two most-watched Excel BBS videos are about inserting bar-shape pictures into cells ([233,296 views](https://youtu.be/UloBqaMiCZs); [155,187 views](https://youtu.be/CxHc8MewWI0)). A printable per-member sheet with shape sketches matters more than a phone UI.

Who it is not for: fabricators who need machine files (BVBS, PEDAX, Schnell), BIM shops that model rebar first, and the Android calculator audience. Those are served by RebarCAD, Tekla and free apps respectively, and none of them pays for an Excel round-trip.

## Demand: a Rs 3.5 lakh crore flow argued over in Excel at 5 to 9% wastage

The volume side is not in doubt. Finished steel consumption was **152.1 MT in FY25** ([Ministry of Steel](https://steel.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/Final%20Annual%20Report%202025-26%20(English%20Version).pdf)) and **163.7 MT in FY26**, with World Steel forecasting 7.9% and 8% growth in 2026 and 2027 ([IBEF](https://www.ibef.org/industry/steel)). Bars and rods, the closest official proxy for rebar, were 63.07 MT in FY25 ([JPC](https://jpcindiansteel.nic.in/writereaddata/files/TrendReportApril%202025.pdf)); at a Rs 55,000 per tonne mid-point ([HeadsUpB2B](https://www.headsupb2b.com/blog/tmt-bar-price-today)) that is about Rs 3.5 lakh crore a year, and every tonne of it passes through someone's BBS. Buyer counts are harder: 1.38 lakh projects registered under RERA since 2017, roughly 17,000 a year, with Maharashtra holding 40% and Pune the top city ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/budget/news/india-real-estate-growth-2025-rera-gst-infrastructure-125013101263_1.html); [Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/119-lakh-real-estate-projects-registered-under-rera-in-top-10-states-propequity-report-549417)), and CREDAI represents 13,000+ developers ([CREDAI](https://www.credai.org/abouts)). Central capex of Rs 12.2 lakh crore and a PMAY-Urban allocation of Rs 18,625 crore for FY27 keep the infrastructure and affordable-housing share growing ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/budget/budget-2026-fm-sitharaman-raises-capex-for-fy27-to-122-lakh-crore); [Rustomjee](https://www.rustomjee.com/blog/budget-2026-27-overview-of-real-estate-and-infrastructure-provisions/)). There is no verified national BIM mandate ([InfraLens](https://infralens.in/handbook/bim-india-snapshot); [Autodesk 2025](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/pdfs/autodesk-bim-roi-report-2025.pdf)), so Excel remains the deliverable of record for the forecast period.

The labour side shows the job is a standing line item but cheap. Indeed lists about 1,000 quantity surveyor and billing engineer vacancies ([Indeed](https://in.indeed.com/Quantity-Surveyor-Billing-Engineer-jobs)) and Glassdoor 226 BBS-engineer jobs ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/engineer-bbs-jobs-SRCH_KO0,8_KE9,12.htm)); a QS earns Rs 3.65 to 7.1 lakh a year ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Salaries/quantity-surveyor-salary-SRCH_KO0,17.htm)), a BBS is sold on Fiverr for $50 to 100 ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/engr_imtisal/make-detail-bar-bending-schedule)), and a school-building BBS averaged Rs 5,750 across 11 bidders on Freelancer ([Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/excel/school-building-bar-bending-schedule)). A BBS engineer at Rs 35,000 a month costs about Rs 1,600 a working day, which caps what any per-seat price can be.

The money is in the error, not the labour. Steel is 25 to 40% of RCC structure cost ([Construction Estimator India](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/what-is-the-cost-of-rcc-per-square-foot-with-material/); [Comaron](https://www.comaron.com/blog/rcc-frame-structure-cost-per-sq-ft-india-2026-complete-guide)). The one peer-reviewed Indian case, Embassy Manyata in Bangalore, cut wastage from **3.36% with manual BBS to 0.53% with software**, saving 23.42 t and Rs 12.9 lakh on about 860 t, against a software spend of Rs 2.3 lakh of which AutoCAD was 83% ([IJT 2015](https://ijtonline.com/HTML_Papers/International%20Journal%20of%20Technology__PID__2015-5-2-44.html)). A May 2026 account of a G+18 tower in Thane with 1,150 t of steel found the director believed wastage was 3% because "3 percent is what his estimator adds in the BOQ", while reconciliation against the BBS showed 8.4%; "steel usually lands between 5 and 9 percent against the theoretical bar bending schedule" and "half the wastage arguments in this industry are actually two people measuring two different things" ([Sitely](https://sitely.in/blog/construction-material-wastage)). A Coimbatore apprentice reported "26MT steel was found missing" at reconciliation ([Civil4M](https://civil4m.com/threads/steel-reconcillation.7547/)). Missing anchorage at supports "is where most steel-quantity disputes during billing originate" ([InfraLens](https://infralens.in/knowledge/bar-bending-schedule-tutorial-is-2502)). One percentage point of national wastage on 63 MT is about Rs 3,500 crore a year.

Two cautions for the demand story. First, the visible audience is trained to pay nothing: a BBS basics video has 1.66 million views ([YouTube](https://youtu.be/nGoTdkCxsuk)), templates sell at Rs 249 ([Construction Estimator India](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/?p=16811)), and the leading app monetises under $10,000 a month. Willingness to pay sits with firms, which is why the segment table above starts with the developer. Second, the specific pain RebarCalc solves, format mismatch between contractor and client sheets, is not written about publicly because every firm treats its sheet as internal; the only hard evidence is the two client workbooks already in `data/`, including a DRA Ld table that is 40D under a header that says 50D. That is a research gap to close with five customer conversations, not a reason to doubt the pain.

**Product-market fit, honestly.** RebarCalc has problem-solution fit in two accounts and zero market evidence beyond them. What is proven: exact reproduction of a Provident slab sheet (23.790617 kg) and a DRA beam sheet (34,227.2066779835 kg over 369 beams), schedule-to-BBS generation with doubts, and write-back with live formulas. What is not proven: that a third, fourth and fifth house format import without new code; that a developer will pay for an audit tool; that a contractor will pay for a generation tool. The gate to call fit is simple to state. Five more house formats imported with no change to the calculator, and one RA bill passed without a steel query because the client's checker used the same numbers.

## Competition: nobody round-trips the client's workbook, and the incumbents hide their prices

The product being web-ified is older and smaller than the brief assumed. Newton Software Pvt Ltd is in Pune, incorporated 2005, and sells AutoScan (BOQ from AutoCAD) and AutoSteel (cutting lengths and BBS from AutoCAD into Excel) as Windows desktop add-ons by demo and WhatsApp, with no public price, no web version and a review footprint of one G2 review and five Techjockey ratings ([newtonindia.com](https://newtonindia.com/); [Tofler](https://www.tofler.in/newton-software-private-limited/company/U72900PN2005PTC021011); [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/autosteel-enterprise)). Its moat is relationships with developer estimation departments, not product. The rest of the Indian field is the same shape: CADS RebarCAD India in Chennai (L&T, Tata Projects, stock-bar optimiser, inside AutoCAD, quote only) ([CADS India](https://www.cadsindia.com/rebarcad/bar-bending-schedules/)); Ensoft BarBeQue in Mumbai, established 1996, standalone with IS 2502 shapes and a cutting module, quote only ([IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/proddetail/bar-bending-schedule-and-quantity-estimation-software-21141483630.html)); Bendplus, a cut-and-bend plant ERP that imports Excel BBS for production, tonnage-tiered, no amounts ([Bendplus](https://bendplus.com/pricing/)); and Reinforcement BBS, a one-office Mumbai SaaS whose pitch is "Replace Excel for faster & accurate BBS" with no published price or customer ([reinforcement-bbs.in](https://www.reinforcement-bbs.in/)). The only Indian list prices found were Rs 1,01,000 a year for Exact Rebar via a reseller snippet ([IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/spectrocad-technologies)), RGS Rebar at $75 a month ([Viskartech](https://www.viskartech.com/global/products/rgsrebar/)) and a grey listing at Rs 1,000 a month ([IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/software-for-bar-bending-schedule-bbs-2854592891530.html)).

Global tools are priced for a different buyer and presume the rebar is modelled first: Tekla Structures at Rs 95,500 to 6,11,300 a year per seat via an Indian reseller ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/ordernow/tekla-bimsight)), Allplan at EUR 3,156 a year ([Allplan](https://www.allplan.com/rebar-software/)), aSa and Cubicost quote-only ([aSa](https://pdf.directindustry.com/pdf/asa-applied-systems-associates-inc/estimating/4608631-1103393.html); [PSG listing](https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/images/psg/201102124D_20240118_30012025_Desensitised_Annex3_Part4.pdf)). The new money is AI takeoff for North American estimators: Togal at $299 per user per month ([Togal](https://togal.ai/pricing-licenses)), Kreo at $35 to 175 ([SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/kreo-2d-takeoff/pricing)), Beam AI at about $8,000 a year ([datadrivenaec](https://datadrivenaec.com/tools/attentive-ai-beam)), Rudus (YC, June 2026) arguing that general tools "treat concrete as a single line item" ([Rudus on HN](https://nuxt-hackernews-elgap-me.pages.dev/item/48374528)), and iRebar at $25 to 500 per takeoff with a +91 phone number ([iRebar](https://irebar-ai.vercel.app)). The closest new threat is **InstaBOQ**, launched on Product Hunt on 2 November 2026 by a solo Indian maker: upload PDF drawings, AI reads beam elevations and slab schedules into a BBS and BOQ, flags beams it may have misread, exports Excel, and is free, with one upvote so far ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/instaboq)). It is an AI-first version of RebarCalc's roadmap with no evidence of house-format parity.

Positioning follows from the table. The one Indian cloud rival chose "replace Excel" and has no visible traction; RebarCalc should say **"keep your Excel, make it exact"**. The doubts panel has no equivalent in any Indian tool and is the same idea the 2026 AI entrants are selling as "estimator stays in control"; lead with it. And because every incumbent is quote-only, a published price is itself a differentiator and a cheap demand test.

## Price per house format and per floor, publish it, and sell through the format owner

The price anchors in this market, lowest to highest: Rs 0 for an Excel template; Rs 200 to 650 per tonne for an outsourced BBS ([IndiaMART](https://dir.indiamart.com/impcat/rebar-detailing.html)); about Rs 5,750 per freelance job ([Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/excel/school-building-bar-bending-schedule)); Rs 1,000 a month for a grey-market BBS seat; Rs 12,000 to 48,000 per user per year for construction ERP; $75 a month for RGS Rebar; Rs 1.03 lakh a year for AutoCAD; and "contact us" for every BBS incumbent. Powerplay's experience is the warning at the bottom: 40,000 firms on a free app produced Rs 4.39 crore of revenue and a 57% valuation cut ([RPrealtyplus](https://www.rprealtyplus.com/index.php/article/powerplay-hits-40000-firms-reinvents-indias-construction-management-playbook-123269.html); [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/powerplay-raises-2m-amid-57-valuation-drop)). Small contractors do not convert; the mid-market developer, PMC and Class I contractor do.

| Model | Price band | Fits which segment | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per house format, annual, for the format owner | Rs 60,000 to 1,50,000 per format per year, unlimited contractors on that format read and export free | Developer or PMC estimation department | The format is the asset; the client pays, contractors adopt because the checker uses the same numbers. | Per tonne | Rs 50 to 100 per tonne scheduled | Mill BBS cells, cut-and-bend centres, detailers | Mirrors how they already bill and scales with their throughput |
| Per seat | Rs 2,000 to 5,000 per month | Contractor billing teams that want unlimited use | Below one engineer-day a month; above this the buyer hires instead |

Recommendation: lead with the per-format licence to developers and PMCs, and the per-floor price for contractors who arrive via a developer's format. Publish both. Everyone else in India hides the price, so a public one is free marketing and a demand test at once.

The go-to-market motion is one workbook at a time. The sales unit is not a seat but a house format, and the proof is "send us your issued BBS, we return it exact, with every assumption listed, within 48 hours". Land the two accounts already in `data/` (Provident, DRA) as paying design partners and get their column and footing sheets. Then work western India first: Maharashtra holds 40% of RERA registrations, Pune is the top city, and Newton's customer base is there, so a displacement pitch ("the same Excel, in a browser, no AutoCAD seat") lands on warm ground. Channels that reach the deciders at low cost are the third-party quantity auditors (CQRA and peers, who already tell developers to verify bar by bar), the QS training ecosystem whose regional videos pull 30,000 to 60,000 views each in Tamil, Telugu and Hindi, and the WhatsApp groups those channels run. Mill BBS cells and ReadyBuild centres are a later partnership once new formats import without code. The message, in the brand's own register: your format, your formulas, every assumption flagged, 34,227.2066779835 kg to the gram.

Measure five things: new house formats imported with no calculator change; percentage of cells matching the client's cached values on import; doubts per hundred bars and how many get a ruling; hours from workbook received to BBS returned; RA bills that pass without a steel query. The first is the product-market fit gate, the last is the sales story.

Risks worth naming. The format long tail could turn the company into a services shop if every import needs code; the mitigation is making the importer configurable before the fifth account, not the fiftieth. A free AI entrant (InstaBOQ today, a mill or Powerplay tomorrow) could make generation worthless; the defence is exactness and the audit trail, which get more valuable as generation gets cheaper. The mills already give BBS away with cut-and-bend steel; RebarCalc's answer is that a buyer-side check of the seller's BBS is precisely what a mill will never supply. And the data today lives in one browser's localStorage, which no developer's IT will accept for a project of record; cloud sync is a sales prerequisite, not a feature.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the product. RebarCalc is not a BBS calculator competing with 189,000-install apps or a detailing tool competing with RebarCAD; it is a format compiler that lets two parties who distrust each other's kilograms agree on them, and the party that owns the format is the one that should pay. That makes the sales unit the house format rather than the seat, makes the developer's head of estimation rather than the contractor's engineer the first call, and makes the reconciliation statement rather than the BBS itself the feature that justifies a monthly invoice. It also reorders the roadmap: tonnage coverage (columns) and the argued kilogram (reconciliation) come before cleverness (AI extraction), and the doubts panel, which looked like a UX nicety, is the defensible asset once a free AI reads drawings for everyone.

The open questions are all answerable with five conversations, not more desk research: whether a third and fourth developer's workbook imports without code, whether an estimation head will pay Rs 1 lakh a year to audit contractors in their own format, whether a contractor will pay per floor to be accepted first time, and whether anyone has ever lost an RA bill argument over 40D versus 50D. Until those are asked, every number in this brief is market context; after them, it is a plan.
